The paired capability harness re-run on 8175be5, two-node, 2026-08-19.
=====================================================================

Prefill went 1213.8 -> 174.3 ms over the day before this. Every step of that was
validated by a tolerance against our own previous lane or a single "does it emit
sensible English" check, and neither can answer whether the model is still as
capable. This is that measurement, taken again.

Four sections, in the order the questions arise:

  1. REPEATS      the same binary on the same ids, four times. It is bit-
                  identical, so a disagreement below is a property of the model
                  and not of the run. (On 2026-08-13 this was NOT true: the flip
                  rate at low margin was 8.55%, and one of eight scored answers
                  changed between two runs.)
  2. ISOLATION    the two items that disagree, across the pipeline split and
                  across the two binaries. Today's run differs from the 08-13
                  run in two ways -- a day of optimisations, and a 21/21 two-node
                  split in place of a 20/22 pair over loopback -- and one
                  comparison cannot separate them.
  3. OLD vs NEW   both binaries in the SAME two-node configuration, 14 items,
                  logits rather than argmax. Argmax is a step function over items
                  whose margins are mostly several logits; it stays flat through
                  a lot of drift.
  4. DIRECTION    whether the drift moved the lane toward the BF16 oracle or
                  away from it.

The headline (paired_open_2node_8175be5.txt) is 38/40 agreement, 1/36 loss,
McNemar p = 1.0000 -- against 40/40 and 0/36 on 2026-08-13.
